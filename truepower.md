# The TRUE power of hypermedia
The connection of everything you know to everything else that you know, easily referenced by humans, not AI. That's what it's good for, and can be used by anyone, not just the researchers at ARC or the malicious sleuths at the FBI. Information wants to be free, because otherwise ignorance takes over and is taken advantage of by tyrants.
## NLS: A Leap in Scientific Study
The NLS wasn't meant to keep track of people, save for scientific endeavors such as studying what causes mood swings or reconsideration. It's not just science either: it's for public information, private information, anything you wanted to study. It was the beginning of understanding tons of stuff in little time.
### The Beginnings of Hypermedia for the User
Hypercard was the beginning of it all, and it was competed against by Aldus Supercard which was more of a corporate product. It was designed by one of the Apple Lisa and Macintosh visionaries, Bill Atkinson, and was born as a way for people to quickly create databases, programs and control centers for all sorts of things. It was provided for free with each purchase of any model Macintosh, from the Plus to the Macintosh 2, but its manual came with a separate purchase of the product.

I personally use it to create a variant of my knowledge base here on Windows. Because of its ability to be programmed a lot, I can really go all out with all the information I have on hand, cross-referencing as much as I possibly can without going so overboard as to get utterly lost. This is what inspired the internet. Then after Web 2.0 got infested it went downhill from there. Everything is controlled and sold by corporate, including your own private data.
### Modern Problems Require Modern Solutions
Of course, that isn't to say there are no longer any good hypermedia platforms. I made my first e-zine in Decker, a spiritual successor, which I think still isn't complete, though it's almost done. Many people make e-zines in various formats, helping the old style stay alive. Of course, it's mostly artistic work, but it can still be used to tie together scientific research for example.

It's in these scenarios that such things would excel. Nowadays only indie enthusiasts and corpo developers know how to make this available for the user. Even that is fading away with the abuse of "artificial intelligence".

### Hypermedia in education
It was no surprise to see that educational sectors took big advantage of this. Same with companies like Microsoft. It was absolutely everywhere, in an aesthetic now dubbed the Utopian Scholastic. It recently got a revival actually, back in 2017 or so.

It was a great help for children who felt that the unified school system was not helping at all. Indeed, including computers in education started in the mid-late 1970s. Not only did the Apple II have LOGO, a teaching programming language, tons of other software was made for it specifically for the educational market.

It simply happened that multimedia was absolutely perfect for the educational situation at hand for the schools.
