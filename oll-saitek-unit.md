# One-liner log: Saitek Cyborg/Gamer Command Unit
The Saitek Cyborg and Gamer Command Units (I think they're the same under the hood[?]) present a unique opportunity for this project of mine: the ability to proceed with my work on the go. The whole thing can be tightly compacted for storage or travel purposes, and is freely adjustable thanks to hand-controlled screws similar to the thumb screws you see on PC cases of the past 40 years. Its only drawback is the inability to switch profiles on the press of a button. It has to be switched with a literal 3-way switch.

### Gaming in general
To be updated, but the thumbstick looks excellent for use as a D-pad. Likely digital.

### As a data processing companion
To be updated, but less buttons means less things I can do, so any complex stuff I have to do will need to be done at home. I'll have to decide which ones are more important to me when I go out there.
