# Raider concept (RaidersSphere), how it can work
I was thinking about the Raider fighters of the RaidersSphere doujin flight combat series. Fighters so simple its pilots are called "Drivers", and easy to maintain for small teams of people, like the company Ellea works for.

Small fighters like these can be used for all sorts of things, not just fighting (though they can fight if needed). Their lower heat output as a result of not needing a big thruster and being lightweight results in them being hard to lock on to by thermals alone, granting a pseudo-camouflage at long range and forcing regular fighters to get in close with guns and closer than normal missile shots.

If I were to make Raiders for people, they would be cheap to produce and easy to refine. Anyone who wants some basic training to ensure they can fly can grab a certificate of proficiency, and can renew it at any time if they feel rusty. People who want to use command nets from their tower can do the same.

Aviation is expensive, even as a hobby. Most hobby planes don't even come with weapons preinstalled, and installing them is illegal in most states (in flagrant violation of the Constitution), especially due to licensing tribulations. Mine won't have that issue, and I'll shake up the world of aviation in the process. If the gun is the great equalizer, then a small missile or two would keep people from acting stupid and give criminals a pants-wetting fright.
