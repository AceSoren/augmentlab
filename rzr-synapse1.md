# Razer Synapse 2: The hidden Offline Mode
Whew! I didn't know Razer had this up their sleeve before they ditched it for "Guest Mode" which still siphons your data regardless of any attempts on your end to avoid it. The online requirement is still very much there in Razer Synapse 3 and above. Now to talk about it and preserve this information for future me in case I forget!

This here configuration file is found at ```C:\Users\You\AppData\Local\Razer\Synapse\RazerLoginData.xml``` as a plaintext file that doesn't get parsed for authenticity. I feared the oncoming update at first, but I didn't have any sweat as it was just drivers for all supported hardware like the Tartarus or this here Razer Blackwidow Chroma, one of the few pieces of hardware that has lasted throughout my pops' time spent gaming. In, fact, I'm writing this section up with this on my lap!

Only minor hitch is that one of the rubber feet are missing. I can get a replacement set on eBay. Otherwise I can borrow the foot that came off my N52 years ago, until I *can* get it replaced LOL!

The only info you need to give is the first time you log in, nothing else. With this file and this particular version of the Synapse 2 installer, I won't give Razer another data-based dime. Time to see about the old Tartarus V1 or V2, though the N52 is doing me just fine.
