# Half-empty or Half-full: a small dissertation on psychology
This is a redux of an old blog post that I wanted to turn into a proper page in my knowledge base.
## Why this question now?
It seems to expose a major weakness in the modern way of thinking taught in schools. I'll explain more. However, the issue is that most think it must be answered.
### How most people answer
I would make a long story short, but this needs explaining. The way most people answer is either "half-empty" or "half-full", and often the answer is also a struggle to reach. That's because most people don't want things left inconclusive, and are used to answers beign reached immediately thanks to the corporate internet.
### Well, what else is there?
It's an easy educated guess that the question could be about how you think or a test of one's emotional disposition. For me, back in my blog post, I made the logical answer that it's contextual, whether the cup is filled or empty to halfway would have decided my answer. As a question to learn one's emotional leaning, I would've said it was half-empty, seeing as water is something to drink, and that it would be an insult to fill it only halfway.

It's such a fascinating question. A lot of psychological research can be done with this question alone. However, I would be remiss to permit myself to manipulate others for my own gain using underhanded tactics unless it was an abject requirement for survival (and certainly not when I'm the bad guy). That would make me just as bad as the people I oppose, both naturally in life and in terms of morals.
