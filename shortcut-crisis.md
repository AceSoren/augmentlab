# The issue with a boatload of shortcuts
Most programs that haven't optimized their UI to corporate spec by now are often inundated with shortcuts, especially when it's an all-in-one suite like Unity, Godot, DaVinci Resolve or even Shotcut, my main video editor. I have personally found it difficult to get to rapid prototyping in engines like Godot exactly because of this when projects get larger than a few scripts. The problem is often solved by making macros.

Full-size keyboards like my Razer Blackwidow Chroma (Synapse 2 offline FTW) have the ability to be remapped almost entirely, giving them the edge in these complex tasks with macros out the yin-yang. However, even they can't always resolve the complexity of such tasks when you're on the clock. Another thing that hampers speed is the UI; the programs involved usually aren't designed to handle the use of a mouse a ton. Lists and rectangles do not mesh too well, even if we have used them for decades.

However, it's the best we got. I'll keep using my keypads and this keyboard to the best of my ability to reach out to others with wisdom and faith. Under God's Guidance.
