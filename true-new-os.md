# Developing a truly new operating system
## Wait, what!? Why a new operating system? You're already using Linux, right? Isn't that enough?
For most cases, yes. However, I agree with Apple's move to disrupt the x86 market with their new ARM64 M series and a complete overhaul of their OS. If we are to break free from the corporate shackles completely, a new OS, whether made by a small company or a FOSS initiative, can help us retreat from the constant guerilla warfare we FOSS enjoyers usually face, especially when it's FOSS. It also allows us to explore new territory with what we know already, and better implement 

The hobby operating system sphere has already shown us that respectable systems can be made, whether they're Unix-like or completely custom like KolibriOS or SkiftOS. However, I have some requirements, like helping others publish their software on my platform. It could also be considered protestware because I will not be applying any sort of API, not since I'll be breaking away from the current paradigms completely.
### Where I got this idea
It was when I was researching the oN-Line System[^1] after stumbling across it on Youtube. It struck me as so odd that the information on how to make a similar environment was not democratized, I thought that everyone has the right to store, examine and publish information... That's when it hit me.

At first I was set on making a similar environment to it on Windows, using my Belkin Nostromo N52[^2] as the master shortcut device. Then I looked at the world of hobby systems. I could double-whammy it by making this environment in a brand new OS, or at least a respectable hobby OS after some deep thought. I guess that deep thought is because I know I'm out of my league. That just means I need to study and learn all the harder.
### The OS itself
It would have to be 32-bit, there's no way around it. Whether it's ARMv7 or i386 to i686. For publishing purposes, I want to make it so that when you have something to install, a text or graphical wizard will do the installing for you. That means I need a unified dependency structure, and all programs will need to come with their own dependencies, to be used by that program alone to avoid easy startup fails.

### Wait, I know you from that inactive Raynix project. Is this the same?  
No, it's not. It's going to be smaller in scope, and Raynix is going to be a HEAVILY modified Linux, but I hope that whether I take it back up or if someone else runs with it, it will shake the confidence of the interlopers. This is something entirely new, something I hope will bloom into an environment that will suit the needs of people from many walks of life, minus military use.
### What about age verification  
NO.  
  
More seriously, I am fed up with taking up the lesser of two evils. I absolutely will not comply to any age verification nor ID verification laws. Not at the OS level, certainly not at the service level will I permit this. Neither this new OS nor Raynix will EVER comply, nor will we permit a change of license on Raynix's side which is GPLv2 because of its basis in Linux.
