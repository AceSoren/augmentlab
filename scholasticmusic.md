# The curiously perfect antidote of Utopian Scholastic aesthetics
## Wha-
I know, I know, this sounds weird, just stick with me a moment here eh? There was a time and day where we were ecstatic about learning of the world around us. That wasn't some coincidence, educators around the world wanted to get children interested in the arts, sciences, mathematics and so on. This is how it turned into the Utopian Scholastic style.
### Whimsy and Wonder through Multimedia
It was the 90s and early 2000s, and the technological boom had brought about a sense of amazement and belief in the future. Atheists, Christians and Buddhists alike were wanting to explore the new world of science and computing, a world where public information was at your fingertips. It was a world where you could publish your own works for all to see in a world flooded with unique cultures.

You'd have a globe on your desk to reference locations you'd see online. You probably have a copy of Microsoft's Encarta or Desktop Brittanica installed to have an offline archive of the world's knowledge. Maybe you were visiting the Scholastic bookstore at school that day, with all the whimsy of a future Macross pioneer and a desire to learn everything that fit your fancy.

It wasn't just books. It was digital images and video. It was indeed books. It was programs that let you cross-reference to your heart's content. It was everything you could ask for if a traditional school environment wasn't doing it.
### In the days before 9/11 WRECKED IT ALL
It was indeed a time of faith in the future, where everything was positively blooming with a positive and peaceful outlook. People dreamed of going to space to see the great unknown, the final frontier. Others sought to explore the depths of our oceans, as they too contain great mystery. Many believed that life on Earth would only get better from here. That is, until the Twin Towers collapsed. It was such a shock that most were paralyzed, only to be taken advantage of by a government that believed that peace was not profitable enough.

Nowadays, war and corruption plague many of us, while weird things get put in our minds and we can't seem to cooperate with each other at all. Attempts to do so result in new problems created by our governments that keep us divided. It makes me want to spread the whimsy of Utopian Scholastic to get everyone to stop for a sec and wonder what the fuck they're doing. Personally, it drives me to learn everything I can for the sake of a better future.

### A potential hitch in the gears
Back in the day, "gifted" children like me were subjected to various projects like FLAG and GATE. We were tested on psychic capabilities and drank weird pink liquid that fucked with our memory of the day to day. Some of us got our memories back sooner, but people like me are only recovering information about this stuff now. This kind of puts a dampener on my experience with Utopian Scholastic aesthetics, but nonetheless, I'm glad I can now become a better man after decimating the Sealing Gate.
### Back to the Aesthetics - MUSIC!
The music used in this style is an eclectic combination of all sorts of electronic, music from various cultures, and a combined sense of unity in that everyone could live with each other in peace with their own cultures. It was almost always peaceful, whimsical and full of wonder. Sometimes it gets ever so slightly darker for important moments in human history that are less than stellar. When you were a student of the era, you would be able to learn all this and more so long as you went looking and exploring. Everything except government secrets was available to you, and if you needed government secrets for whatever reason, there were various ways of retrieving it.

### What does this mean for me?
If you ever felt like you could never move forward in life, or felt that education just flat out sucks, check out some of the stuff that fits the aesthetic, like Microsoft Encarta. Just know that support for a lot of Utopian Scholastic era and style stuff got dropped in the 2010s, so if you wanted to know the latest developments in the modern day, you'll want to kick your verification up a notch and wrestle with the dullness of modernity.

That's not to say you couldn't make something of the aesthetic. I bet you could make an absolutely banger piece educational software if you're a teacher that hasn't fallen into the trap of "get this over with". It's really beautiful what people have made already in honor of the style.

_Well, whaddaya waitin' for!? There's a whole world out there! Explore!_
