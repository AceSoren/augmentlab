# Law vs Morals and Ethics
Now THIS sounds like a conundrum.
## Disconnect
So here's the hitch: we usually make laws to avoid things like rampant thievery or unwarranted death. That's a good thing, right? What happens when those laws turn against the people at the behest of the lawmakers, when obviously bogus laws that are abhorrent to the core inalienable laws of your nation stand just because said lawmakers want it to?

They also obviously ignore the law of the land because no one is willing to enforce it for fear of their lives in a docile society. What's next, complete enslavement? Yeah, basically. We're being paid what amounts to zilch because of all the taxes the government uses to rebuff its losses to inane projects that seek to wipe humanity as we know it off the face of the earth, replaced by an obedient class who does nothing but the government's will.

### Fear
The main tool of the Empire in Star Wars was fear. However, in an attempt to break Princess Leia's spirit, Alderaan gets destroyed by the Death Star on orders from Darth Vader. What bit the Empire in the _ass_ was simply this: fear was naturally replaced by anger and vengeance as the Rebellion turned from a ragtag bunch of misfits to a well-oiled guerilla war machine.

It makes me wonder what would happen if I decided to turn into a heel that antagonizes faces online.
